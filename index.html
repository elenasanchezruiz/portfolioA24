Work

Obsession: Keeping the conversation alive

Obsession was set to arrive in Spain on June 26, more than a month after its release across other European territories. By the time it reached Spanish audiences, there was a risk that the initial excitement would have faded—or worse, that moviegoers would already know too much about the film. We needed to reignite curiosity and make people want to experience Obsession for themselves.

After ruling out ideas ranging from a true-crime angle to a real-life case involving public figure Lydia Lozano, we decided to turn the existing enthusiasm into the creative itself: instead of telling people why Obsession was worth watching, we wanted to show them what happened when they experienced it firsthand. Working alongside the Publicity, Trade and Promotions & Partnerships departments at Universal Pictures International Spain, we organised a special screening attended by 400+ guests, comprising press, content creators, winners of UPI social media contests and representatives from the Sitges Film Festival.

In collaboration with audiovisual production company Muchobambū, we captured their genuine reactions during the screening using infrared cameras, allowing us to document the moments that would otherwise remain unseen. We then interviewed top content creators as they left the screening room, capturing their immediate thoughts and emotional responses. Together with my colleagues in the Digital department, I helped develop the idea and shape the resulting content for its digital and paid media rollout, turning those authentic reactions into a campaign designed to draw fresh attention to the film.

Over a 72-hour paid campaign running across Instagram Reels and TikTok Top Feed, this piece generated 2.37M impressions and reached 1.51M users around the Spanish release, bringing Obsession back into the conversation at a key moment. With a €6.5K investment, the campaign delivered CPMs of €2.82 on Instagram and €2.59 on TikTok. As part of the wider marketing efforts surrounding the film, this activation contributed to the overall campaign that supported Obsession in surpassing €9M in box-office revenue in Spain.

Forgotten Island: Turning friendship into an experience

Forgotten Island was a new DreamWorks title with no existing franchise behind it. Its unique animation style and its deeper mature themes meant that its audience was more specific than kids, the typical animation crowd. We required a way to introduce the film to the right public while making friendship, its central theme, feel tangible.

The concept evolved through several iterations: from an initial Spotify Stage to a Soulmate Party featuring Spanish singers and best friends Belén Aguilera and Samuraï, and ultimately to an interactive landing page built around a friendship test inspired by Forgotten Island’s universe. Users could take the test with a friend, and the highest-scoring pair would win a trip to Sardinia.

Alongside my colleagues in the Digital department, I helped define the creative concept, selected the visual materials for Bluecell, the agency developing the landing page, and contributed to writing the test questions. To extend the two-week digital activation beyond the online experience, we hosted a special screening where attendees were invited to take the friendship test themselves. Produced in collaboration with Somos Ergo agency, the event brought together 420+ guests, from press and content creators to UPI social media competition winners, Cheerz, TAI and U-TAD students.

Generating over 22M impressions, more than 75K site visits and 500 user registrations, the activation transformed a campaign idea into an immersive digital experience. Paid media assets ran across Meta, TikTok, Direct and Programmatic around the film’s opening weekend, driving traffic to the landing page and giving audiences a new way to step into the realm of Forgotten Island.

The Odyssey: Bringing a global campaign to Spain

Localising The Odyssey campaign assets for Spain implied navigating a complex production and approval process without compromising the original creative direction. With Christopher Nolan's involvement in the campaign, every detail demanded careful review before publication. One example was Scenic Stacks, an international asset that needed to be adapted to the Spanish market while meeting local platform requirements and strict compliance guidelines.

Working within the Digital department, I coordinated the localisation across Universal Pictures International Spain, our Head Office in L.A. and both international and local production partners. I checked with the Creative department which elements required localisation, consulted with the Dubbing department to verify dialogue translations, and liaised with Head Office's international production agency to obtain editable project files with separated image and audio tracks. Then I briefed our local production company Vivavision on the adaptations, secured the dubbed dialogue with our local sound studio Iyuno, and supervised the localisation through to the final asset.

Published across Instagram and TikTok, Scenic Stacks reached a potential audience of 1.4M and generated 40.1K+ interactions. It was one of several localised assets supporting the broader marketing campaign behind The Odyssey, which has surpassed €35M in box-office revenue in Spain, making it Universal Pictures International Spain's highest-grossing film of all time.
